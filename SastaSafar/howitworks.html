Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sasta Safar safe for travellers?

Yes! We verify drivers and passengers through their registered mobile numbers and email addresses before they can post or book trips.

How do I pay for my trip?

You can pay the driver directly using cash or UPI (like Google Pay or Paytm) once you meet at the starting pickup point.

How do drivers decide the seat price?

Drivers calculate the price based on fuel expenses and distance. The goal is to share travel costs fairly, not to make commercial profit.

Can I cancel a booked trip?

Yes, you can cancel your booking anytime before the trip departure. Please inform the driver as early as possible so someone else can book.

What if the driver cancels the ride?

If a driver cancels a ride, you will be notified immediately, and you can easily find and book another available trip on the same route.

Can I offer a seat if I am travelling by car?

Absolutely! Any registered driver with empty seats can post a trip by providing the route, date, time, and seat price to share fuel costs.
