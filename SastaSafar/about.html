About Sasta Safar

Sasta Safar is a community-driven ride-sharing and carpooling web platform built to make intercity travel affordable, safe, and eco-friendly.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To help everyday travellers save money on transport while enabling drivers to share their fuel expenses by offering empty seats in their vehicles.

🌱 Eco-Friendly Travel

By carpooling and sharing rides, we reduce the number of vehicles on the road, cutting traffic congestion and decreasing vehicle carbon emissions.

🤝 Community Driven

Connecting students, working professionals, and frequent commuters across cities to make travel friendly, social, and cost-effective.

College Project Overview

💻 Tech Stack

Built cleanly with standard HTML5 semantic tags, responsive CSS3 styling, and pure Vanilla JavaScript without relying on heavy external libraries.

⚡ Key Features

Interactive trip search & filtering, dynamic seat booking, ride posting form, client-side form validation, and local storage state persistence.

🎓 Academic Purpose

Developed as a practical MCA college project demonstrating fundamental concepts of web development, user experience design, and DOM manipulation.
